# Roadmap

All items below are planned, not implemented. Owners can be assigned once both collaborators have repository access.

## Phase 0 — policy and examples

- [ ] Walk through one past movement, one earnings cycle, and one historical model section; classify each step as automate, assist, or analyst-owned.
- [ ] Resolve pilot-blocking decisions in [decisions.md](decisions.md).
- [ ] Choose the stack, authorized data sources, hosting, and notification channel.
- [ ] Establish a pilot roster and baseline efficiency/learning measures.

## Phase 1 — movement pilot

- [ ] Add holdings, team membership, and owner management.
- [ ] Implement market adapter and validated, aligned observations.
- [ ] Implement versioned movement policy and deterministic trigger tests.
- [ ] Add persistent investigations, event deduplication, and factual notification delivery.
- [ ] Build evidence workspace with source locations, timestamps, notes, and unresolved questions.
- [ ] Add deadlines, safe reminder retries, completion records, and audit history.
- [ ] Replay a known movement in dry-run mode and conduct a one-team pilot review.

Exit: a team can follow a qualifying event from validated prices through sourced investigation and analyst completion, with reliability and access acceptance criteria met.

## Phase 2 — earnings

- [ ] Company-approved checklist and chart configuration.
- [ ] Confirmed versus estimated dates and Fund deadlines.
- [ ] Prior documents/questions and preserved analyst expectations.
- [ ] Sourced actual/prior-year/guidance/estimate tables with disclosure-change flags.
- [ ] Approved chart refresh and post-earnings reflection.

## Phase 3 — historicals

- [ ] Review Historicals Solver and agree an integration contract.
- [ ] First-period mapping and rationale supplied by an analyst.
- [ ] Proposals across several periods for the same company, with complete provenance.
- [ ] Exception review, formula preservation, approval and change history.
- [ ] Proficiency-based automation and source-tracing samples before broader expansion.

## Phase 4 — six teams and continuity

- [ ] Reuse shared collection and Fund policy across six team contexts.
- [ ] Permission-aware research with public/team origin and working/approved status.
- [ ] Human routing for unanswered questions.
- [ ] Semester handoff of sources, mappings, and unresolved questions.

## Suggested two-person split for the pilot

One collaborator can own ingestion, deterministic rules, and delivery reliability; the other can own the workspace, analyst workflow, and pilot evaluation. Agree schemas together and review each other's pull requests. These are suggested workstreams, not assigned roles.
