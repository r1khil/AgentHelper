# Product scope

## Purpose

Reduce time spent gathering information while ensuring analysts can trace every number, explain model mappings, and defend their conclusions. The approved baseline is recorded in decisions.md; it supersedes conflicting proposals in the original outline.

## Ownership boundaries

| Workflow | Agent prepares | Analyst owns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Major movement | Aligned return calculation, factual notification, sourced possible catalysts, questions, reasoning feedback, ownership and deadline tracking | Catalyst assessment and final major-movement email |
| Before earnings | Confirmed/estimated date, prior documents, approved charts, prior questions | Expectations, key questions, and thesis-change criteria |
| After earnings | Sourced actuals/comparatives/guidance/estimates, chart updates, disclosure flags | Interpretation, assumption changes, and post-earnings update |
| Historicals | Proposals using approved company-specific mapping, provenance, exception flags | First-period mapping and rationale, exceptions, approval before model writes |
| Cross-team research | Permission-filtered retrieval with origin, date, and research status | Team opinions and approval of team positions |

The workspace contains sources, extracted facts, charts, unresolved questions, and analyst notes. Do not introduce a draft-update button or detailed generated interpretation that effectively completes the analyst's assignment.

## Later-stage requirements

Earnings work preserves pre-earnings expectations and invites post-earnings reflection. Dates identify whether they are company-confirmed or estimated. Sources arrive independently; missing transcripts or estimates remain visibly missing.

Historicals proposals carry period, units, currency, reported label, source location, and derivation. Review quarterly versus year-to-date values, fiscal versus calendar periods, GAAP versus adjusted measures, reporting and segment changes, restatements, signs, and units. Model formulas must survive approved writes, and changes must be auditable. Greater automation follows demonstrated proficiency, with occasional source-tracing checks.

Six team experiences share collection and Fund policy infrastructure. Cross-team retrieval distinguishes public sources from approved team research and working views from approved positions. Private notes retain their existing permissions. Unanswered questions can be routed to a person. Preserve sourced history, mappings, and open questions across semesters.

## Success measures

Track gathering time, missed deadlines, false and duplicate alerts, source accuracy, and corrections to historical inputs. Team leads also check source tracing, mapping explanations, event reasoning, and how evidence affects the thesis. Establish baseline measurements before claiming improvement.

## Proactive team experience

One shared application maintains dedicated team workspaces and persistent holdings, theses, peers, prior updates, and open questions. Require holdings and owners, flag missing context, and require analyst approval for stored thesis changes. Never infer team investment positions from chat alone.

Agents initiate qualifying closing-movement investigations and daily briefings on material developments and upcoming catalysts. Suppress empty briefings. Email links bring analysts into the workspace. Agents can explain concepts and suggest sourced possible catalysts; after analysts provide reasoning, agents flag unsupported claims, missing evidence, alternatives, and thesis contradictions and request revisions. Analysts retain authorship of the final argument and conclusion.

Access is invite-only: members see their team work, Fund administrators can access all teams, and cross-team sharing requires explicit approval. Evaluate evidence explanation, fact/hypothesis distinction, and defensible thesis implications alongside preparation time and unsupported claims. Collect brief feedback after completion.
